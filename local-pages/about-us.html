About TrueFocus 5D Studios

About Us

TrueFocus5DStudios is a pioneering AI studio transforming ordinary 2D videos into studio quality immersive 3D experiences​. Founded in 2024 as a passion project in the Washington, DC area – famously starting with two $50 projectors duct-taped together and a DIY silver screen for early 3D demos​ – TrueFocus has since evolved into a leader in Long Sequence Stable and High Quality 2D to 3D conversion. Our team has developed sophisticated multi-model and multi-agent AI pipelines that convert 2D video content of all types into Full 3D VR Experiences.

What Sets Us Apart?

Solving AI Depth Challenges

 

Unlike one-click AI converters that often struggle with accuracy, our multi-pass approach was built to overcome the common flaws of AI depth tools. Many AI-based 2D–3D solutions rely on a single-pass depth estimation, which can misjudge scene geometry and yield inconsistent or incorrect depth maps. Applied naively frame-by-frame, these methods tend to produce distracting flicker and continuity errors in long videos. They also frequently introduce visible distortions—such as warping, halos, or “long-nose” artifacts around objects—especially in complex or high-contrast scenes. TrueFocus5DStudios attacks these issues head-on with a multi-agent, multi-pass pipeline designed for stability, clarity, and fidelity.

​

Multi-Agent Depth Estimation

 

Instead of depending on one neural network to do it all, we deploy multiple specialized AI models (agents) in tandem. Each agent focuses on different aspects of the scene—from recognizing tricky textures and edges to tracking objects across frames—and collaborates to build a superior depth map. This division of labor means errors that might fool a single general-purpose model get caught and corrected. The result is a far more accurate and robust depth estimation at every frame, as demonstrated by recent research: multi-frame and iterative approaches can achieve more reliable, state-of-the-art depth results on long videos.

​

Multi-Pass Refinement

 

Our conversion doesn’t stop at one pass—it continuously improves itself. In initial passes, we generate a baseline depth map for each frame; subsequent passes then refine and align these depth maps using temporal cues and additional context. By comparing frames over time, our system eliminates the jitter and flicker that plague single-frame methods. Even competitors have recognized the need for such techniques—such as introducing models with temporal smoothing to stabilize depth in video. TrueFocus’s pipeline was architected with this stability in mind from the start. Long sequences remain smooth and consistent, no matter how complex the motion or camera cuts, so an entire film can play out in 3D with cohesive depth.

​

Minimal Artifacts, Maximum Detail

 

We’ve solved the notorious artifacts that often come with 2D–3D conversion. Traditional 3D conversion algorithms tend to stretch or extrapolate neighboring pixels to fill gaps when creating the stereo pair, which leads to obvious “halo” distortions around foreground objects. Our multi-pass pipeline takes a smarter approach—it leverages information from adjacent frames and employs AI-based inpainting only when necessary to fill in missing pieces with realistic detail. In other words, we use real image data whenever possible instead of inventing content. This greatly reduces warping and smearing in the final 3D imagery. The payoff is a crystal-clear stereoscopic image—backgrounds and fine details remain intact and natural, without the fuzzy edges or double-images you might see in lesser conversions.

​

Proven Consistency on Challenging Footage

 

TrueFocus5DStudios prides itself on handling footage that breaks other depth AI tools. Fast action scenes, complex lighting, and long-duration shots are routinely problematic for single-pass AI converters, often requiring manual touch-ups for over half the scenes in a movie. In contrast, our system thrives on these challenges. By analyzing each scene over multiple passes (and even leveraging scene-specific AI agents when needed), we maintain depth precision and stereo alignment where others falter. We’ve successfully converted full-length films with zero need for post-fixups, delivering a uniform high-quality 3D experience from the opening scene to the end credits. This level of consistency simply isn’t attainable with most “auto-3D” apps today.

​

Near-Studio Quality, Accelerated

 

Our unique pipeline marries the best of AI automation with techniques used in professional studio conversions. The outcome is an automated process that produces 3D content approaching the artistry of painstaking manual conversions—but in a fraction of the time. TrueFocus outputs are industry benchmark in quality for AI-driven 3D: no other one-click tool offers the same mix of sharpness, depth realism, and temporal stability that we do. And we achieve this while supporting the latest formats (SBS, top/bottom, MV-HEVC, etc.) and resolutions needed for today’s AR/VR displays.

 

At True Focus we deliver cutting edge 2D to 3D conversion—fast, scalable and with true cinematic quality. That’s what sets TrueFocus5DStudios apart from the rest of the pack, and why our 3D pipeline is trusted for projects where quality is paramount.
